# Executive recommendation release — 7 October 2026

Base: 457d1d3. Scope: independent Federal Market Position, Phase 1.

## Product contract

- A reconstructable evaluated basket gets a Recommended PTW and Competitive Corridor. Absent prices become separate bounded assumptions, never fabricated source facts.
- Preserve actual quantities, options, extensions and specified components. No invented scope, ceiling division, universal discount, duplicate burden or fee.
- Decision confidence is Strong, Moderate or Limited with reasons and practical use guidance; it is neither a win probability nor an opportunity score.
- The executive view answers price, reason, confidence, sensitivities and next action. Evidence, scenarios, source conflicts and calculation machinery remain in the analyst workspace and workbook.
- An unreconstructed quantity/formula is distinct from a missing price: retain the known-scope subtotal and explain the specific missing basis. This does not pass executive acceptance.

## Numerical judgment

For labor, choose among observed applicable rate economics according to the source-selection pressure and role qualifications, scarcity and evidence quality. Broadly supplied qualified roles may use observed efficient rates under price-ordered evaluation. Specialist, cleared and uncertain mappings protect central economics. Missing rates use a duty-consistent occupational analogy or an explicitly labeled bounded engineering estimate. Engineering estimates are LOW confidence hypotheses, not independently researched rates. Unit-price lines preserve every extracted evaluated quantity. No company costs or winning prices are asserted.

Normalized comparable awards independently challenge the basket; mismatched awards are not averaged into it. Competitive field evidence affects confidence, not an invented rival bid. Corridor endpoints include the stated lower/upper input conditions. Material unresolved economics and source disagreements lower confidence. A ready deployment does not imply credible procurement-specific judgment.

## Known defect prevention

Contract Year 1 cannot be calendar year 1. Bound period lengths and escalation factors before multiplication/export. Preserve atomic source rows and round extended rows to cents. UI, PDF, Excel and saved-run reopening recompute one authoritative decision.
